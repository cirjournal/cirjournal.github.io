Welcome

Advancing innovation through rigorous, accessible research

The CIR Journal of Innovation & Applied Research (CJIAR) is a peer-reviewed, open-access, multidisciplinary scholarly journal published by the Centre for Innovation & Research (CIR). The journal aims to promote innovative research, practical solutions, and evidence-based knowledge that contribute to sustainable development, technological advancement, policy formulation, and societal progress.

CJIAR serves as an international platform for researchers, academicians, practitioners, policymakers, and industry experts to disseminate high-quality original research, review articles, case studies, technical notes, and innovative applications.
