Submit a Manuscript

Submit your work to CJIAR through our straightforward online process.

What to Submit

Authors should prepare the following four components before submitting.

1

Main Manuscript

The full manuscript without author identification (for double-blind review).

2

Title Page

Author names, affiliations, and corresponding author details.

3

Cover Letter

A statement of originality and the significance of the work.

4

Ethical Approval

Required where applicable (human/animal studies).

Online Submission Process

  1. 1

    Prepare your files

    Follow the Author Guidelines for formatting and structure.

  2. 2

    Complete the checklist

    Verify all pre-submission requirements are met.

  3. 3

    Email your submission

    Send your files to the editorial office (see below).

  4. 4

    Receive confirmation

    You will receive an acknowledgement and a manuscript ID.

  5. 5

    Peer review begins

    Your manuscript enters the double-blind review process.

Submit by Email

Send your complete submission package to the editorial office.

submissions@cirjournal.org

Please include "Manuscript Submission – [Your Topic]" in the subject line.
