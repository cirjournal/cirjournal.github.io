Journal Policies

The policies that govern access, copyright, archiving, and corrections at CJIAR.

Open Access Policy

All articles published in CJIAR are freely accessible to everyone, immediately upon publication, without financial, legal, or technical barriers. We follow a Gold Open Access model.

Copyright Policy

Authors retain full copyright of their published work. By publishing with CJIAR, authors grant the journal the right to publish and disseminate the article while maintaining ownership of their intellectual property.

Archiving Policy

CJIAR ensures the long-term digital preservation of all published content through:

  • Institutional Repository
  • LOCKSS (planned)

Corrections & Retractions

CJIAR maintains formal correction and retraction procedures to preserve the integrity of the scholarly record. Errors identified after publication are addressed transparently, and articles that breach ethical standards may be retracted with a published notice.

Questions about our policies?

Contact the editorial office and we'll be glad to help.
