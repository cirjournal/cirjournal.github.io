Author Guidelines

Everything you need to prepare and submit a manuscript to CJIAR.

On this page

Manuscript Formatting

File Format
Microsoft Word (.doc / .docx)
Language
Clear English (UK or US, applied consistently)
Length
4,000 – 10,000 words
Including references and tables
Layout
Times New Roman, 12pt
1.5 line spacing · 1 inch margins

Manuscript Structure

  1. 1 Title — concise title, author names, institutional affiliations, and corresponding author email.
  2. 2 Abstract — a single paragraph (150–300 words) outlining the research problem, methodology, key findings, and practical implications.
  3. 3 Keywords — 4 to 6.
  4. 4 Introduction
  5. 4 Problem Statement, Rationale of the Study (optional)
  6. 5 Literature Review Focusing the Research Gap
  7. 5 Objectives
  8. 5 Conceptual/Theoretical Framework(where applicable)
  9. 5 Research Methodology
  10. 5 Research Questions/Hypothesis (optional)
  11. 5 Result/Analysis
  12. 5 Discussion & Findings
  13. 5 Recommendations (optional)
  14. 9 Conclusion
  15. 10 References — APA 7th Edition referencing.

Plagiarism Limits

  • Overall similarity: less than 15%
  • Single source: less than 3%

ORCID Requirement

Authors are encouraged to provide ORCID IDs for accurate attribution and discoverability.

Pre-Submission Checklist

Before submitting, authors must ensure:

RequirementStatus
Original work, not previously published
Not under consideration elsewhere
All authors approved submission
Ethical standards met
Language is English
Format follows template
References complete

File & Document Format

Single file
MS Word (.doc/.docx)
PDF submissions
Accepted
File size
Maximum 15 MB

Required Document Structure

  1. Title Page — full title (max 20 words), author names with affiliations, corresponding author contact, keywords (4–6).
  2. Abstract — English abstract 150–250 words, structured (Background, Methods, Results, Conclusion).
  3. Main Text — Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusion.
  4. References — APA 7th edition, minimum 15 references, include DOI for all sources.
  5. Tables & Figures — numbered sequentially, captions included, high resolution (300 dpi minimum).
  6. Supplementary Materials (if applicable) — data files, additional figures.

Author Information Required

For each author, provide:

Full name Affiliation (institution, department, country) Email address ORCID ID (if available) Corresponding author designation

Language Requirements

  • Primary language: English
  • For non-English manuscripts: include an English title translation
  • Professional English editing is recommended for non-native authors

Reference Format Examples

Journal Article (APA 7th)

Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of article. Title of Journal, Volume(Issue), pages. https://doi.org/xxxx

Book (APA 7th)

Author, A. A. (Year). Title of book. Publisher.

Submission Requirements

  • Complete manuscript in MS Word
  • Figures embedded or separate high-resolution files
  • Tables in editable format
  • Supplementary data (if applicable)
  • Cover letter (optional but recommended)

Ethical Requirements

  • No plagiarism (self or others)
  • No data fabrication
  • All sources acknowledged
  • Conflict of interest disclosed
  • Ethical approval for human/animal studies
