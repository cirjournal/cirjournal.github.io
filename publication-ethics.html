Publication Ethics

Upholding the highest standards of integrity in scholarly publishing.

Our Ethical Foundations

CJIAR follows the ethical principles of leading international organizations:

COPE

Committee on Publication Ethics

ICMJE

International Committee of Medical Journal Editors

DOAJ

Directory of Open Access Journals

Authors' Responsibilities

  • Original work
  • Proper citation
  • No fabrication
  • No falsification
  • No duplicate publication

Editors' Responsibilities

  • Fair decision making
  • Confidentiality
  • Conflict-free assessment

Reviewers' Responsibilities

  • Confidentiality
  • Objectivity
  • Timely review

Misconduct Policy

CJIAR takes firm action against all forms of research and publication misconduct, including:

Plagiarism
Data Manipulation
Image Manipulation
Citation Manipulation
Authorship Misconduct

Ethical Review Board

The journal maintains an Ethics Committee to:

  • Investigate ethical concerns
  • Handle complaints
  • Recommend sanctions
  • Update ethical policies

Whistleblower Protection

Anyone reporting ethical violations is protected from retaliation. Reports can be submitted confidentially to:

ethics@cirjournal.org
