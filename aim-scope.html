Aim & Scope

The multidisciplinary reach of CJIAR and the types of research we publish.

Our Aim

The CIR Journal of Innovation & Applied Research aims to:

  1. Publish high-quality, original research on innovation and applied sciences and application-oriented research that contributes to academic advancement and practical problem-solving.
  2. Provide an international forum for academic and industrial researchers.
  3. Promote state-of-the-art research in innovation and applied technology.
  4. Facilitate knowledge exchange between academia and industry.
  5. Support research development in Bangladesh and globally.

Publication Frequency

  • 4 issues per year (Quarterly)
  • Submission deadlines: March 1, June 1, September 1, December 1
  • Publication timeline: 6–8 weeks after acceptance

Geographic Focus

  • Primary: Bangladesh (with emphasis on local research)
  • Secondary: International (global submissions welcomed)
