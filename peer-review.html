Peer Review Policy

A rigorous, transparent double-blind review process that safeguards research quality.

Review Model

Double-Blind Peer Review

Both authors and reviewers remain anonymous to each other throughout the review process, ensuring an unbiased and fair evaluation of every manuscript.

1

Initial Editorial Screening

Scope, formatting, and suitability check by the editorial office.

2

Plagiarism Check

Mandatory similarity screening against published sources.

3

Reviewer Assignment

Minimum two independent expert reviewers are assigned.

4

Editorial Decision

Accept, minor revision, major revision, or reject.

5

Final Acceptance

Production, formatting, and open-access publication.

Possible Editorial Decisions

Accept
Minor Revision
Major Revision
Reject

Review Timeline

Initial Screening
7 days
Review Process
3–6 weeks
Final Decision
6–8 weeks

Reviewer Confidentiality

Reviewer identities remain strictly confidential throughout and after the review process. This protects reviewers and ensures candid, objective feedback for every submission.
